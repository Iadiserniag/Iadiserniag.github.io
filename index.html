About Me

I am a PhD student in Data Science at Sapienza University of Rome. My research focuses on assessing the trustworthiness and explainability of large reasoning models, particularly for applications in economics and finance. I am a member of the RSTLess research group and a research intern at the Bank of Italy.

Short Bio

I graduated summa cum laude from LUISS Guido Carli University with a BSc in Management and Computer Science and earned my MSc in Data Science at Sapienza University of Rome. I completed multiple internships at the Bank of Italy, contributing to projects in NLP, LLMs, ML, and monetary policy. My academic work includes publications in AI in Finance and authorship analysis in central bank communications.
