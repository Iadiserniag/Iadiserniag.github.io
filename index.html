Giulia Iadisernia

PhD Candidate in Data Science

About Me

I am a PhD student in Data Science at Sapienza University of Rome. My research focuses on assessing the trustworthiness and explainability of large reasoning models, particularly for applications in economics and finance. I am a member of the RSTLess research group and a research intern at the Bank of Italy.

Bio

I graduated summa cum laude from LUISS Guido Carli University with a BSc in Management and Computer Science and from Sapienza University of Rome with an MSc in Data Science. During my studies I developed a strong interest in the intersection between data science and economics.

During both my bachelor's and master's studies, I collaborated with the Applied Research Team (ART) at the Bank of Italy. My bachelor's internship project focused on the structural properties of the Bitcoin Lightning Network using graph theory. I later returned for my master's thesis internship, where I worked on authorship attribution and topic analysis in Italian central bank communication using natural language processing methods.

In 2025 I joined the Applied Research Team (ART) of the Bank of Italy as a research intern working on large language models and their applications in macroeconomic forecasting. This work led to the publication Prompting for Policy: Forecasting Macroeconomic Scenarios with Synthetic LLM Personas, presented at the ACM International Conference on AI in Finance (ICAIF 2025).

I am currently a research intern at the Directorate General for Economics, Statistics and Research of the Bank of Italy, where I am developing NLP-based methods measure hawkish–dovish sentiment of monetary policy communication and construct an index of perceived monetary policy stance. Part of this work is available through the project ECB Textual Tone Index. This internship follows my selection as "particularly deserving" in the Giorgio Mortara scholarship competition.
